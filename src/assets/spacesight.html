What can SpaceSight do for you?
  • AI Instant Sensing

    Edge-embedded VLM with hybrid edge-cloud processing. Millisecond response time. Zero-code one-click deployment. Hardware cost reduced by 60%.

  • SpaceClaw Agent

    Built-in space intelligence agent. Natural language video search and command execution. Autonomous learning, adaptation, and closed-loop task completion.

  • AI Audit

    Zero-shot universal detection, no training data needed. Works immediately on new scenarios. Complex instruction parsing unlocks 20× audit efficiency.

  • Intelligent Reports

    Dynamic card design boosts data utilization by 80%. AI operational reports generated automatically. Every report becomes an insight for growth.

  • The Store's Super Brain: 20× Audit Efficiency

    See everything. Catch everything. Automatically.

    • On-device VLM: Built for large, complex environments. AI algorithm
      channel capacity increased 60%.

    • Natural language configuration: Define audit rules in plain English,
       no code required. (e.g., "Check Aisle 3 shelves every 90 minutes.")

    • Proactive Loss Prevention: Real-time detection of checkout
      anomalies, unauthorized warehouse access, and suspicious
      loitering — with instant alerts before incidents escalate.

    • Privacy by default: VLM deployed on-premise. Data never leaves
      your network. Millisecond response times.


  • SpaceClaw: Find Any Moment in 10 Seconds

    Ask questions about your space. Get answers in seconds.

    • Semantic video search: Describe what you're looking for in natural language. SpaceClaw finds the exact footage clip in seconds.

    • Rapid Incident Investigation: Find footage of theft, inventory damage, or workplace incidents in seconds — cutting investigation time dramatically and resolving disputes faster.

    • Autonomous task execution: Detects anomalies (e.g., trash bin left open) and automatically dispatches corrective tasks to the store manager's phone.

    • AI operational recommendations: Automatically generates audit scores and improvement plans. Identifies efficiency gains. Helps boost sales-per-square-foot.

  • AI Audit Copilot: Immersive Management

    Audit every location. Every day. No one needs to be there.

    • Zero-shot universal detection: No training data needed. Works immediately across retail, F&B, healthcare, pharma, showrooms, and 30+ complex environments.

    • Zero missed issues: SpaceClaw audit checklist with one-click AI scoring. Real-time risk alerts. Nothing falls through.

    • 20× efficiency leap: From manual CCTV review to AI-powered automatic anomaly detection. Free your operations team for higher-value work.

  • AI Reporting Center: Zero-Wait Decisions

     Your daily store report, ready before you finish your coffee.

    • Automated report delivery: Daily, weekly, and monthly operational reports generated and delivered on schedule. No waiting, no manual compilation.

    • Multi-dimensional analysis: Combines audit compliance rate, foot traffic heatmaps, out-of-stock rate, and more into one structured report.

    • Dynamic card dashboard: Next-generation interface — key KPIs visible at a glance. Data utilization increased 80%.

    • SpaceClaw recommendations: Beyond reporting — the AI agent delivers specific action recommendations: restock alerts, staffing optimization, loss prevention, traffic redirection.

  • AI Security & Loss Prevention

    Catch theft before it costs you. Protect every corner, automatically.

    • Checkout loss prevention: AI detects suspicious behavior at the register in real time — misscans, item concealment, unauthorized discounts. Alerts triggered instantly.

    • Warehouse & stockroom monitoring: 24/7 AI surveillance on high-value storage areas. Detects unauthorized access, unusual movement, and inventory discrepancies automatically.

    • Behavior anomaly detection: Identifies loitering, restricted area entry, after-hours activity, and other risk patterns across any camera feed — no manual review needed.

    • Evidence ready in seconds: SpaceClaw retrieves the exact footage clip on demand. One search, instant playback. No scrubbing through hours of recording.
