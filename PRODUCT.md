# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS — chosen for a lightweight, dependency-free marketing site that can deploy directly to static hosting without a build step.

## Users

Primary: overseas B2B enterprise buyers and operations leaders evaluating AI platforms for physical locations. They need to quickly understand the product suite, proven use cases, compliance posture, and how to engage commercially. Secondary audiences include tech media, channel partners, and conference referrals who form a first impression of the brand.

## Product Purpose

An English-language corporate website for Whale, an AI operations platform that converts camera footage, voice conversations, and floor activity into structured, real-time intelligence for enterprise operations. The site must generate interest, establish credibility, and drive demo requests without feeling like a conventional industrial B2B catalog.

## Positioning

A unified platform that sees, hears, remembers, expresses, and thinks about physical operations — fusing edge video (SpaceSight), voice intelligence (Echo), AI assistant orchestration (Alivia), knowledge operations (Harbor), and content distribution (Lume) into one operational intelligence layer.

## Operating Context

- Visitors typically arrive from search, event landing pages, or outbound sales links.
- Decision makers compare use-case fit, compliance certifications, reference deployments, and deployment speed.
- The sales cycle is consultative; the website’s job is to earn a demo request, not to close transactions.
- Content will be consumed on desktop (presentation rooms) and mobile (conference floors, commute).

- Light, lively, vivid: avoid dark, heavy, “industrial brochure” aesthetics.
- Approachable but credible: bright color, generous whitespace, friendly motion, and modular cards rather than dense spec tables.

## Product Principles

1. **Feel like a product brand, not a parts catalog.** Lead with outcomes and integration stories, not datasheets.
2. **Vivid clarity over industrial sobriety.** Color and motion serve comprehension, not decoration.
3. **Every section earns the next scroll.** No filler “about us” prose; each block proves a claim or invites action.
4. **Built for the buyer’s context.** Desktop presenters and mobile scanners must both get the point in seconds.
5. **Leave no factual claim unlabeled.** Synthetic data is clearly marked so the team knows what to replace.
